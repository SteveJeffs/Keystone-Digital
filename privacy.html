Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: this site collects nothing about you unless you fill in a form, and what you send is used only to reply to you. Here's the full version, still in plain English.

Who I am

Keystone Digital is run by Steve, based in Wallington, Surrey. For anything in this policy, contact me at [EMAIL].

What this site collects

If you fill in the contact form or the free mockup form, I receive what you typed: your name, your business name, your email address, your phone number if you gave it, your website address if you gave it, and your message. That's the complete list. The site has no user accounts and no payment processing.

What this site doesn't do

This site doesn't use analytics, tracking scripts or advertising pixels, and it doesn't set any non-essential cookies, which is why there's no cookie banner asking for your consent. There's nothing to consent to.

Why I collect it and what I do with it

Form submissions are used for one thing: replying to you about the thing you asked about. The lawful basis under UK GDPR is legitimate interest: you contacted a business, the business writes back. I don't add you to a mailing list, I don't send newsletters, and I never sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.

Where your information goes

This site is hosted by Netlify, and form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify's form service, then emailed to me. Netlify acts as a data processor; their own privacy policy is at netlify.com/privacy. Beyond that, your details live in my email inbox.

How long I keep it

If we end up working together, your details are kept as part of normal business records. If we don't, I delete enquiry details within twelve months.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask me what information I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email [EMAIL] and I'll sort it, no forms, no runaround. If you're not happy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If anything on this site changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if I ever add analytics, this policy will be updated before it happens, with the date below.
