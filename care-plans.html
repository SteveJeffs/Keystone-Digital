The terms, plainly

The terms: three months, then monthly

Every plan runs for a minimum of three months, then rolls monthly. The three months aren't a trap: they cover the period after launch when you see the site live, have new ideas, spot the things you'd change, and I make those changes without either of us watching the clock. After that, cancel whenever you like. Just tell me before your next payment date and it ends at the close of that month. When you cancel, your site drops to hosting only at £10 a month so it stays live, or if you'd rather take it elsewhere I'll hand everything over. No exit fee either way, and the site stays yours in full because it always was. Unused update time doesn't roll over month to month, which is what keeps the price where it is. If a job's bigger than your plan covers, I'll say so before touching it and quote the difference in writing.

Do you need one?

An honest answer: not always

Already got a domain or hosting you're happy with? No problem. The plan works exactly the same on top of it, you just won't need the hosting that comes with it.

If your site is a simple one-pager and nothing about your business changes, you can skip the plan and just pay me by the job when something needs doing. I'd rather tell you that than sell you a subscription you don't need. Most multi-page sites do earn their keep on a plan, though, the maths of one missed enquiry usually settles it. Unsure? Ask me and I'll tell you which side of the line you're on.

Already got a site someone else built?

Care plans aren't just for my builds. If your current site is unloved, I'll take a look and tell you honestly whether it's worth maintaining or replacing.
