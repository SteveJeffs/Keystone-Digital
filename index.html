How it works

Run like a job, not a project

The same way any decent tradesman works: look at the job, quote it in writing, do the work, stand behind it.

Step 01

We talk, you get a quote

A quick chat about your business and what the site needs to do. Then a fixed, written quote: the price, the pages, the timeline. No obligation, no chasing you for weeks.

Step 02

I build your homepage first

A deposit books the work, then your homepage gets built before anything else. It's the page that matters most, so it's the page you judge me on.

Step 03

You approve it, I build the rest

Only once you've seen your homepage and said yes does the balance become due. Then the remaining pages get built and refined with you, round by round.

Step 04

Launch, and I stick around

Your site goes live on your own domain, owned by you. A care plan from £25 a month covers the hosting and keeps it updated, backed up and current.

The guarantee

You don't pay the balance until you've approved your homepage

Here's the deal, plainly. A deposit books the work, that protects my time. I then design and build your homepage, and you don't pay a penny of the balance until you've seen it and approved it. That protects your money. If you look at your homepage and it isn't right, you've found that out before you're committed to the rest, not after. No web company can fairly ask you to pay in full for something you've never seen. So I don't.

After launch

A website isn't a one-off purchase

It needs updating when your prices change, backing up in case something goes wrong, and keeping current so Google keeps sending people your way. My care plans start at £25 a month, include the hosting, and exist so your site stays an asset instead of slowly turning back into a liability.
