Services

What I actually do, in plain English

Every service below has a real price attached, either here or one click away on the pricing page. If you're not sure which one you need, ask me and I'll tell you straight, including if the honest answer is the cheaper option.

New build

Website design

From £495

A website designed and built for your business from scratch, not a template with your logo dropped in. We work out what the site actually needs to do (usually: make the phone ring), and every page is built to do that job on any device.

Who it's for

Businesses with no website, or nothing beyond a social media page. Trades, salons, clinics, cafés, gyms, small firms. If your customers are local, this is for you.

What you get

  • A bespoke design you approve before the balance is due
  • Pages that load fast and work properly on phones
  • Enquiry forms that land in your inbox
  • Set up properly for local search from day one
  • Your own domain, owned by you, not held hostage by me

See the Starter, Standard and Premium packages for exact scope and pricing.

Rebuild

Website redesign

From £995

You've got a website, but it's ten years old, slow, broken on phones, or just embarrassing. I rebuild it on modern foundations, keeping what works (your domain, anything that already ranks) and replacing what doesn't.

Who it's for

Businesses whose site is actively costing them work. If you find yourself apologising for your website, it's a redesign.

What you get

  • An honest review of what's wrong with the current site
  • A rebuilt site on your existing domain, no address change
  • Careful handling of pages Google already knows about
  • The same homepage-approval guarantee as a new build

Redesigns are quoted as Standard or Premium depending on size.

Small & sharp

One-page websites

£495 fixed

One well-made page: who you are, what you do, where you work, how to reach you, and a form that works. For a lot of local businesses that's genuinely all a website needs to be, and I'll tell you if it's all you need.

Who it's for

Sole traders and small businesses that need to exist properly online without paying for pages they'll never use.

What you get

  • A single page designed around your business, not a template
  • Contact details, opening hours and an enquiry form
  • Fast, mobile-first, set up for local search
  • Easy to grow into a bigger site later without starting over

This is the Starter package, with full scope on the pricing page.

Be found locally

Google Business Profile setup

£95, included with Standard & Premium

When someone searches “plumber near me” or “nail salon Sutton”, the map listings get the clicks. I set up or fix your Google Business Profile so your hours, photos, services and reviews are working for you instead of sitting empty.

Who it's for

Any local business. Honestly, this is the highest-value ninety-five quid in the whole list if your profile is currently a ghost town.

What you get

  • Profile created or claimed, categories set correctly
  • Hours, services, photos and description sorted
  • Linked properly to your website
  • A simple routine for gathering reviews, handed to you

Included free with the Standard and Premium packages.

After launch

Care plans

From £25/month

A website needs looking after: content kept current, backups kept safe, small changes made when your prices or hours change. The care plan means you send me a message and it's handled, instead of your site quietly going stale.

Who it's for

Anyone who'd rather run their business than maintain a website. Hosting is included in every plan, and if you've already got hosting or a domain elsewhere the plan still works on top of it.

What you get

  • Your site monitored, backed up and kept healthy
  • Content changes handled for you each month
  • A real person answering: me, usually same day
  • Three-month minimum, then monthly rolling, and if you ever cancel your site drops to hosting only at £10 a month rather than going offline

Full tiers and terms on the care plans page.

Sorted for you

Domain names

At cost, plus £15 a year handling

People ask about this a lot, so it's an option. I'll find, register and renew your domain for you, registered in your name so it's yours outright, and set it up so it just works with your site. You pay what the domain costs plus a small fee for handling it and never having to think about it again.

Who it's for

Anyone who'd rather not open a registrar account, pick between forty confusingly-priced options and remember to renew it in a year's time. If you already own a domain, or want to buy your own, that's fine too and I'll work with it.

What you get

  • Your domain registered in your name, not mine
  • Renewal handled every year so it never lapses
  • Connected to your site and email, ready to go
  • Full transfer any time you ask, no strings

Goes on the written quote alongside your build, or add it to a care plan later.

Look the part

Basic branding

£150

Not full agency branding. The practical version. A tidy-up of your logo (or a simple new one), a colour palette that works on and off the web, and fonts chosen so your site, van and flyers finally look like the same business.

Who it's for

Businesses whose branding was done in Word in 2014, or never done at all.

What you get

  • Logo tidied up or simply redrawn, supplied in every format you'll need
  • A colour palette and font pairing, documented on one page
  • Everything applied consistently across your new site

Available as an add-on to any package.
